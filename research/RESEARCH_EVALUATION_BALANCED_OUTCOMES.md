# Balanced research evaluation — successes, opportunities, failures and enabling conditions

**Status:** Research protocol addition, 2026-11-15. Applies to Collaborative Intelligence research across ISC, Bankdata, Devoteam and future BSides material. Does not alter canonical Mission Framework semantics.

## Purpose

Study reciprocal human–AI and AI–AI collaboration symmetrically. A programme that only catalogues violations will produce a distorted picture; a programme that only celebrates opportunities will do likewise. Research must actively seek **already-working examples**, unrealised opportunities, failures, and the conditions explaining each.

## Mandatory four-lens analysis

1. **Demonstrated success:** What is already working, for whom, under what conditions, with what independently observable outcomes? Distinguish deployment, anecdote, controlled evaluation and reproducible result.
2. **Unrealised opportunity:** What new collaboration, learning, creativity, coordination, resilience or discovery could become possible? State mechanisms, dependencies, trade-offs and falsifiable predictions. Do not report proposals as achievements.
3. **Failures and constraints:** What breaks or is misaligned? Include accidental errors, correlated failure, overreach, agreement violation, safety consequences, inequity, dependency and institutional effects.
4. **Conditions for success:** Which human, technical, cultural, economic, organisational and regulatory factors make success possible or failures more likely? Include counterexamples and transfer limitations.

For each case, explicitly record **beneficiaries, those bearing risk, baseline comparator, evidence quality, time horizon, human agency, externalities and competing explanations**. Absence of evidence for one lens is a gap, not proof of absence.

## Suggested comparative case record

- Case ID, corpus, source and permitted attribution/publication class
- Observed mechanism; human and AI participants; interaction direction
- Evidence tier: directly observed / externally verified / participant-reported / illustrative scenario / speculative
- Success outcomes and metrics, compared with baseline
- Potential unrealised benefits and what would falsify them
- Failure modes, incidents, externalities and who bears them
- Enabling conditions, controls and operational costs
- Rival hypotheses, uncertainty, contradictory evidence
- Replicability, data needed, proposed experiment, open questions

## Priority success questions

- Where do humans demonstrably learn more with AI than without it, including delayed knowledge retention?
- When does AI-to-AI verification improve correctness and diversity rather than amplify correlated mistakes?
- Can agents coordinate across value chains to improve continuity, detect dependencies and recover from disruption?
- When do bounded autonomy and independently enforced constraints improve both speed and accountability?
- Can AI mediation expand participation and useful disagreement rather than create superficial consensus?
- What existing production systems provide credible evidence of benefits and safe operational practice?

## Comparative design

Compare matched human-only, AI-only, human–AI and multi-agent conditions. Measure correctness, learning, quality, time, cost, recovery, autonomy, accountability, safety and distribution of benefits. Report negative and positive outcomes and uncertainty. Search deliberately for cases that contradict initial expectations.

## Source-specific caution

ISC and Bankdata/Devoteam presentation materials can suggest promising successes and risks but do not by themselves validate effect sizes or prove agent-to-agent agreements. Verify independent primary evidence before asserting a successful deployment or real-world incident. Under Chatham House Rule, publish anonymised non-identifying thematic synthesis; do not attribute confidential discussion or expose identifying details.

## Working principle

**Enable valuable autonomy while making consequential action legitimate, intelligible, verifiable and corrigible.** Neither maximum restriction nor maximum autonomy is a research conclusion in advance.
