# Mission Framework — Interdisciplinary scholarly source register

**Status:** Research bibliography v0.1, 2026-11-15. **NOT canonical and NOT a systematic literature review.**  
**Owner:** Collaborative Intelligence research; review by qualified subject specialists required.  
**Purpose:** Traceable, maintainable starting corpus for the philosophical, anthropological, civilisational, religious, ecological and human-development foundations of Mission Framework.

## How to read the register

- **V = verified bibliographic metadata** against the linked publisher, university or primary institutional record during this pass. Verification does **not** imply full-text reading or agreement with conclusions.
- **C = candidate, not bibliographically reverified in this pass**; title/edition/year and bibliographic details must be checked before citation in a publication.
- **N = normative primary document**; identify institution, adoption year and normative status, not peer-reviewed research.
- Primary historical texts require a specified critical edition, translator, language, date range and contextual scholarship before quotation. **Do not attribute present-day categories to historical authors without analysis.**
- `[ID]` is a stable local citation key; preserve keys, log corrections, and never silently upgrade C to V.

## Coverage and exclusion audit

**Dimensions deliberately covered:** biological/cultural evolution; philosophy; religions and secular/nonreligious ethics; Europe/Africa/Asia/Indigenous knowledge; gender and intersectionality; youth/elders; disability/accessibility (needs dedicated primary research); poverty/class; animal and ecological interests; governance; AI and automation; human work; freedom and wellbeing.

**Known undercoverage — do not claim comprehensive diversity:** Latin American philosophy and liberation theology; Pacific/Oceanian traditions; contemporary Indigenous authors beyond North America; disability studies and neurodiversity; queer and trans scholarship; empirical gender studies outside Western contexts; oral traditions with consent/knowledge sovereignty; secular humanism and nonreligion; intra-religious diversity; philosophy of science and epistemic injustice; organizational burnout and ecological cost of AI; East Asian and South Asian primary-language scholarship. Fill these gaps before publication.

**Adversarial perspectives required:** critics of evolutionary psychology and adaptationism; critiques of WEIRD research; critiques of capabilities universalism; critiques of essentialist gender/culture; critiques of romanticizing Indigenous traditions; religious and secular counterarguments; anti-technological determinism; cost and feasibility challenges to universal provision.

## Maintenance contract

1. Maintain one **stable ID** per work and one canonical citation per edition; register translations/editions as linked variants.
2. For each item add: DOI/ISBN/URL, source type, peer-review status, publisher, year, language, geographic/cultural context, disciplines, themes, perspective, verification date, full-text access status, copyright/license, relevance, limitations, competing interpretation, review owner.
3. Keep **metadata verification** separate from **full-text reading**, **critical appraisal**, **empirical replication** and **normative adoption**. None imply the next.
4. For every proposed Mission Framework principle, cite primary passages (page/section), a serious counterargument, and a traceable bridge from source to design choice and test.
5. Record exclusion reasons, sampling bias and provenance. No artificial quota guarantees epistemic quality; seek relevant diversity and local scholarly authority.
6. Review links/metadata quarterly, review contested/high-consequence claims before use, and capture DOI redirects and publication corrections without overwriting history.
7. Respect copyright, confidential materials, Indigenous knowledge protocols, privacy and translation rights.
8. Store machine-readable citation records in BibTeX/CSL JSON or CSV after bibliographic verification; do not manufacture identifiers.
9. Changes proposed here stay in research until OP-001 governance and cross-repository propagation checks are satisfied.
